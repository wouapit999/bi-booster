# BI Booster

## Register

product

## Users

Bouquet Innovation SA's digital marketing team. Access is limited to team members with a `@bouquet-innovation.net` account. Team members use BI Booster to deliver digital marketing services for client companies; clients do not sign in.

## Product Purpose

BI Booster is an internal social growth operating system for planning, creating, publishing, optimizing, analyzing, and managing engagement across TikTok, Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, X, YouTube, and Threads. Success means the team can serve multiple client brands efficiently, coordinate campaigns, and use performance insights to improve audience growth over time.

## Brand Personality

Confident, energetic, growth-focused. The experience should feel distinct and bold, with clear operational feedback and useful signals for the marketing team.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic AI styling and undifferentiated AI product visuals. Avoid presenting clients as users of the product; it is an internal agency tool.

## Design Principles

- Keep the team's multi-client work visible and easy to coordinate.
- Turn publishing and analytics into a continuous improvement workflow.
- Make important status, ownership, and next actions clear at a glance.
- Use AI to support skilled marketing decisions, not to obscure them.
- Keep the interface accessible and usable across screen sizes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Support keyboard navigation, visible focus, semantic structure, readable contrast, reduced motion, and assistive technology.
